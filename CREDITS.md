## Artwork
Fourteen matching AI-generated papercraft illustrations created for WonderQuest with OpenAI image generation, October 2026. No stock photographs or third-party illustration packs are used. Ancient illustrations are speculative reconstructions. Geographic outlines come from map data, not generated images.
